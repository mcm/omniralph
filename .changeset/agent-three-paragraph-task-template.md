The planning methodology defines one task template of three paragraphs ("I want you to …", "Build it by …", "Don't stop until …"), with Story and Fix as kinds of it, in place of the story and fix formats. Its rules put the checkable done criteria and every constraint in the third paragraph, and treat the second as guidance. Triage prompts now include these rules.
