## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** authored for Omniralph.
- Jobs, Ralph and Reviews share one failed-row pattern: the reason on its own clamped row with "Show all", tied to its row for screen readers. It reads like a flight recorder, not a template table.
- The severity ramp is a deliberate system: five chips set from theme tokens and tested to get quieter at every step in both themes.
- Still generic where it was: every page is an H1 over equal-weight cards, Analytics is organised by metric, and `/` opens Repositories.

**Deterministic scan:** `src` has 1 finding (ignored `broken-image`). The snapshots have 3,196 findings over 192 captures, all under `ignore.md` rows, identical across themes; the only deltas come from the new `jobs-targeted` route. The detector found nothing the design review missed, and no new false positives.

**Visual overlays:** not available; the host has no browser.

## Overall Impression

The cleanest round so far: all three round-4 P2s are fixed and every polish fix holds in both themes at both widths. What remains is small and comes from the fixes themselves: a Findings column that mixes words and initials, year-dropping applied to some lists only, and long phone breadcrumbs.

## What's Working

1. **One failed-row pattern everywhere.** `ListErrorRow` + `useListErrorId` on Jobs, Ralph, Reviews and the run's Jobs table; the targeted-job ring covers the reason row.
2. **A severity ramp that reads in order** in both themes, from list chips to the review summary, guarded by `severity-ramp.test.ts`.
3. **Phone layouts carry the key facts:** Jobs targets, the Analytics Rate, labelled host-tool facts and wrapped code suggestions, with no horizontal scroll.
