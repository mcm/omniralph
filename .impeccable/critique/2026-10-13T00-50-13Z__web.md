## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** The panel is now mostly Omniralph's own. These belong to this product, not to an admin template:
- the failed-run alert that names the story and the attempts, and says how to resume;
- the settings confirmation that reads like a deploy preview (old → new, "Affects the next review of 2 repositories");
- mono fact strips and the consequence copy.

What's still generic:
- every page opens with a 48px H1 over a stack of same-weight cards;
- analytics is organised by metric (KPI tiles and charts), not by "is it getting better";
- `/` still opens a configuration page.

**Deterministic scan:**
- **`src`:** exit 2. `broken-image` (test fixture, ignored) and `side-tab` at `globals.css:369`: the targeted Jobs row has a tint plus a 3px inset accent stripe. Side stripes are banned by the craft floor. The tint alone marks the row, so the stripe goes (P2-3).
- **Snapshots:** 2252 findings, every one in `ignore.md` except `nested-cards` ×24. Six analytics charts put a library `ChartContainer` (a framed surface with its own title props) inside a `Card` (`analytics-view.tsx:171,197,227,383,406,436`), drawing a second rounded border inside each card (P2-7).
- **ignore.md updated:** the `cramped-padding` scope now covers the library Badge, Alert, Select, ChartContainer, Metric, Tabs list and dialog footers (same unresolved Tailwind padding). The stale `nested-cards` StatCard entry was removed.
- **What the detector caught that the review missed:** the nested chart frames and the side stripe.
- **What the review caught that the detector can't:** mid-token wrapping, inverted severity salience, and the lost-edit risk.

**Visual overlays:** none. No browser on the host, so nothing was injected.

## Overall Impression

A real step up: 23 → 30, with every P1 gone. The panel is calm, safe to configure and readable on a phone. What's left is the finish: exact values that break mid-token, a severity chip that shouts at the wrong level, list rows that grew tall, and the safe-settings pattern not yet reaching the task and agent editors. The biggest opportunity is one `MonoValue`/`Timestamp` discipline across every table, so the Exact Values Rule is enforced by code.

## What's Working

1. **Configuration safety is UI, not just copy.** `SettingsCard` gives a per-card dirty badge, Discard, and a confirmation that lists old → new; profiles add the affected repositories.
2. **Detail pages lead with the answer.** Failed-run guidance, one fact strip, empty sections collapsed to one line, and review findings in one card.
3. **One phone pattern for lists.** `PhoneMeta` makes Ralph, Reviews, Jobs, Repositories, Tasks and Agents status-first at 390px, with no inline styles anywhere and contrast clean in both themes.
