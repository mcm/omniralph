- **What:** at 1440 the Jobs target reads "/contr…", "fi…#3…", "fi…!3…" (the tail that middle-truncation should keep is itself cut); failure reasons read "No …", "git w…", "The h…"; Ralph runs cuts FIX-130's failure to "The s…"; the fleet's running job reads "fix… /control-pan…". - **Evidence:** `jobs.desktop.dark`, `ralph.desktop.dark`, `harnesses.desktop.light`; `jobs-view.tsx:101-124`, `globals.css:441-471` (`.jobs-target .mono-value-tail { flex-shrink: 1; text-overflow: ellipsis }`), `ListStatus` `list-table.tsx:60-71`, `.list-error` `globals.css:335-341`.
